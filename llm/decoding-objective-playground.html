Prediction checks

Say the answer aloud before opening each panel.

1. With `|V|=6`, how many decoder runs occur at exhaustive-search step 4?
`6^(4−1)=216`. Runs count prefixes entering that step; candidate sequences after the step would be `6^4=1296`.
2. At `τ=.5`, can grape enter top-2 ahead of banana?
No. Division by any positive temperature preserves logit order. Temperature changes gaps and probabilities, not rank.
3. Why is beam width 2 not top-k 2?
Beam deterministically retains two joint sequence prefixes across time. Top-k truncates one next-token distribution to two tokens and samples from it.
4. What fraction of all BERT tokens become literal `[MASK]` in expectation?
`15%×80%=12%`. The loss still covers all selected 15% positions.
5. Why is the off-path GA4 answer not zero?
Zero would assert impossibility. The required later conditional probability is missing, so the supported answer is insufficient information (`-1` where permitted).
6. When should you report MLM sum and when mean?
Follow the question's stated reduction. If it merely says loss, state the convention. The official BERT slide averages over selected positions; the GA key accepts both its sum and mean ranges.
