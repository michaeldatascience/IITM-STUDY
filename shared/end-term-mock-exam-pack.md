# IITM LLM + DLCV End-Term Mock Exam Pack

Last updated: 2026-08-31

## Purpose

This pack is a cumulative resistance test for IITM LLM and DLCV Weeks 1 through 12. It contains three 20-question papers:

- Paper A: LLM Weeks 1 through 12
- Paper B: DLCV Weeks 1 through 12
- Paper C: mixed LLM and DLCV retrieval

The interactive artifact stores drafts, best scores, flags, and the error-led retest queue in browser local storage.

## Source policy

Questions are newly written analogues, not a verbatim question dump. Their tested skills were selected after reviewing:

1. Official week-wise lecture boundaries and the relevant official slide pages
2. All supplied LLM and DLCV End-Term PYQ files
3. Captured LLM bonus and graded assignments
4. Captured DLCV GAs and PAs
5. Existing source-grounded modules and recorded historical weak areas

Where a later assignment had no usable key, it was used only to identify a question pattern. The answer in this pack was derived independently from official course notation.

## Coverage blueprint

| Paper | Questions | Deliberate pressure |
|---|---:|---|
| LLM | 20 | Q/K/V and parameter counts; masks and LayerNorm; CLM; decoding; BERT; tokenizers; BART/T5; scaling and data pipelines; efficient attention; KV inference; RoPE and ALiBi |
| DLCV | 20 | Filtering; Canny and corners; optimization; CNN shape, parameters, receptive field; efficient CNNs; detection and segmentation; RNNs; visual attention; ViT and DETR; GAN, VAE, and DDPM |
| Mixed | 20 | Cross-course switching; transfer of shared attention mechanics; late-week retrieval; CLIP, SimCLR, BLIP, BLIP-2, CFG, VQ-VAE, plus high-frequency numerics |

## Attempt protocol

1. Take a paper closed-book in one sitting.
2. Flag uncertain answers, but commit to an option.
3. Submit before opening notes or modules.
4. Read explanations only after submission.
5. Run the generated retest queue immediately.
6. Re-run that queue after a delay before treating the skill as stable.

## Scoring interpretation

| Score | Interpretation | Action |
|---:|---|---|
| 85-100% | Strong retrieval on this paper | Clear all queued errors after a delay |
| 70-84% | Structure is present but not resistant | Revisit only the weeks represented in the queue, then retest |
| Below 70% | Several exam-critical mechanisms are unstable | Return to the linked canonical modules before another full paper |

Generation is not evidence of mastery. Study status changes only after the user reports a closed-book result.
